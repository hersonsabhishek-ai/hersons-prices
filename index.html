How the last selling price is worked out

Sony: RRP × 93% (KD-32W835 at 96%). Where Sony gives an EOL clearance price, that is used instead of RRP. Sony's list has no NLC, so no margin is shown. Provisional.

Daikin: DP × 93%. NLC is the SD NLC from the Sep 2026 list. Provisional.

LG: Net + 5%, rounded to ₹100. LG lists only Net, so Net is shown as NLC. The 5% is a placeholder.

A red card means the last selling price is below NLC. Sony and LG prices were typed from photos of the price lists, so check the original before quoting a big order.
