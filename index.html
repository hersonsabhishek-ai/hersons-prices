How the last selling price is worked out

Sony: RRP × 93% (KD-32W835 at 96%). Where Sony gives an EOL clearance price, that is used instead of RRP. Sony's list has no NLC, so no margin is shown. Provisional.

Daikin (price list): DP × 93%. NLC is the SD NLC from the Sep 2026 list. Provisional.

LG: Net + 5%, rounded to ₹100. LG lists only Net, so Net is shown as NLC. The 5% is a placeholder.

Daikin bill sets, Voltas, Voltas Beko, Blue Star, Kenstar, Microtek: NLC is the per-unit price on your latest 2026-27 purchase bill (incl. GST). Daikin indoor and outdoor units are added together as one set. Last selling price = NLC + 5%, rounded to ₹100 (₹10 under ₹10,000). The 5% is a placeholder.

Product details (the blue tags) are read from the model number and the price lists: size, capacity, tonnage, star rating and type. A ≈ means the capacity is taken from the model code, so check the box or brochure before quoting it to a customer.

A red card means the last selling price is below NLC. Sony and LG prices were typed from photos of the price lists, so check the original before quoting a big order.
